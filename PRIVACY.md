# CIVITAS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

CIVITAS is a skill that runs entirely inside your Claude session.

- **No data collection.** CIVITAS does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
- **No external connections.** It has no MCP connectors, makes no network requests, and sends nothing to the author or to any third party.
- **Local computation only.** Its Python simulators use only the standard library and run in Claude's own code-execution environment or on your machine (Claude Code).
- **Your conversations.** What you type into Claude is handled by Anthropic under Anthropic's own privacy policy, not by CIVITAS.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/MidnightLabDev/Civitas/issues
