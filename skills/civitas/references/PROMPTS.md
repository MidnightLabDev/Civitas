# Prompt laboratory | copy and adapt

## Literary ideas and research

- Explain Asimov's psychohistory as fiction, then compare it with agent based modeling, causal inference and scenario planning. Include credible sources and disprove the idea of precise historical destiny.
- Build a theme map connecting Foundation, Robot stories, Empire, The End of Eternity, The Bicentennial Man and The Gods Themselves. Separate canon from original design analogies.
- Compare CIVITAS with Project Psychohistory, Concordia, OASIS and Mesa. Explain evidence of real differences and unresolved claims.

## Future systems and strategy

- Use CIVITAS FORECAST and STRESS to explore three conditional futures of private AI infrastructure over 15 years. Identify measurable signals and robust near term decisions.
- Model how a trusted network might become too centralized through its own success. Provide feedback loops, affected stakeholders and a falsification plan.
- Invent three business models from the conflict between individual autonomy and collective coordination. For each, show the first safe pilot.

## Autonomous agents and governance

- Build a constitution for autonomous business agents that can request but not execute real payments without separate approval. Include authority, logging, redress and a misuse test suite.
- Model competing goals of an agent society with shared compute and energy. Propose an incentive model and explain resource conservation.
- Design a persistent civilization game where institutions endure but agent memories can fade. Provide event sourcing, schema, replay and economy specifications.

## Experimental design

- Turn the following speculative claim into a model that could fail. Include alternative hypotheses, measured indicators, baseline, seeded runs and stop conditions.
- Run civlab `society` under none, peer, central and polycentric governance with 10 matched seeds each; report cooperation, Gini and legitimacy with intervals, then a Morris sensitivity on multiplier and corruption.
- Audit this model for ecological fallacy, confounding, false causality, unjustified probabilities, resource inconsistency, game rule exploits and independent human oversight.

## Worldbuilding

- Create a fictional society whose efficient knowledge institutions eventually suppress technological experimentation. Show four centuries of adaptation and one external shock without copying any Asimov plot.
- Build five distinctive civilizations with different governance and resource systems. Show tradeoffs, ordinary lives, and peaceful routes out of conflict.

## One prompt to challenge CIVITAS itself

Use CIVITAS AUDIT to challenge the central premise of CIVITAS. Find where our model assumptions, literary metaphors, forecasting methods or simulations may fail. Design a competitor approach that is simpler and more reliable and propose a real test to tell them apart.

## New in the 1.0 enhancement

- HISTORY: "Compare the Han dynasty's end with the Western Roman Empire's using structural-demographic theory and Tainter. Where does each theory fail?"
- WARN: "Here is a CSV of monthly grain prices for a fictional republic. Is there an early-warning signal of a regime shift? Run `ews` and explain what a null result means."
- GOVERN + SIMULATE: "Design a constitution for 50 autonomous trading agents sharing GPU time, then test rule capture and an entrenched clause in `aisociety`."
- FORECAST: "Build a resolvable question about AI regulation in the Gulf by 2030, give a base rate from a stated reference class, update with three pieces of evidence, and log it."
- WORLDBUILD: "Run `polity` for 300 ticks and turn the trajectory into a five-era history of an original continent, with one ordinary life per era."
- STRESS: "Use `cascade` to compare how a supply network fails under random vs targeted disruption, and propose redundancy that minimizes regret."
- RESEARCH: "For each of the Three Laws, give a modern AI failure mode it fails to prevent and an engineering control that does better."
- AUDIT: "Audit this claim: 'Our 1,000-agent LLM simulation shows universal basic income increases trust.'"
