## Psychohistory research brief

**Question and context:** precise claim and system scale.
**Evidence ledger:** source, inference, assumption and fictional claims separated.
**Underlying mechanism:** actor incentives, network, resources and institutional feedback.
**Competing explanation:** at least one with a measurable distinction.
**Uncertainty and reflexivity:** what cannot be assumed stable.
**Test:** realistic data needed and one falsifiable prediction.
**Sources:** links with publication dates where relevant.
